## First-call hard gates
1. Can you support Ballast → merchant → end-customer distribution?
2. Who funds the principal?
3. Who bears credit loss and what recourse exists?
4. Who is the regulated originator/creditor and contracting party?
5. Who handles servicing, delinquency, renegotiation and complaints?
6. What legal/regulatory role is expected from Ballast and each merchant?
7. Can the journey be white-label or merchant-branded, and what disclosures remain mandatory?
8. What integration options exist: API, hosted flow, webhooks, sandbox?
9. What ticket, tenor, borrower profile and use cases are supported?
10. What are all commercial fees, minimums, reserves, clawbacks, subsidy requirements and exclusivity terms?
11. What is the realistic pilot path and implementation timeline?
