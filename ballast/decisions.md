# Ballast decision log

## 2026-10-11 — Initial operating model
**Decision:** prioritize a distribution / embedded-credit model in which Ballast does not initially fund loans from its own balance sheet.

**Reason:** this better matches Ballast's intended role as merchant-distribution and commercial infrastructure while reducing capital intensity and unsupported credit risk.

**Evidence state:** USER_PROVIDED strategic direction; financial viability remains to be tested.

**Revisit when:** a partner proposal, FIDC structure or economics demonstrate a materially superior alternative.

---

## 2026-10-11 — Initial merchant wedge
**Decision:** focus initial prospecting on high-ticket merchants in architecture, interiors and construction, beginning in Greater São Paulo.

**Reason:** ticket size and project nature create a plausible use case for financing as liquidity preservation and project acceleration.

**Evidence state:** USER_PROVIDED + ASSUMPTION. Merchant-level demand still requires discovery.

**Revisit when:** discovery data shows another segment has better conversion, economics or partner underwriting fit.

---

## 2026-10-11 — Partner due-diligence discipline
**Decision:** no financial partner is treated as launch-ready until multi-layer distribution, funding, risk, regulated roles, servicing, data and economics are confirmed.

**Reason:** a high marketing-level fit can still fail on one structural hard gate.

**Evidence state:** OPERATING PRINCIPLE.

---

## 2026-10-11 — Evidence language for merchant financing status
**Decision:** do not state that a prospect “does not offer financing” unless directly verified. Use “no dedicated financing solution identified in public sources reviewed” when appropriate.

**Reason:** protects prospecting quality and prevents false claims.

**Evidence state:** OPERATING PRINCIPLE.
