# Ballast Competitors

## Scope
Map competition at multiple layers because Ballast is a system, not one product.

### Layers
1. Direct distribution/embedded-finance platforms serving similar merchants.
2. Financial infrastructure/CaaS providers Ballast could partner with or compete against.
3. Merchant-side substitutes: card installments, bank financing, store credit, consortia, BNPL, manual referrals.
4. Consumer-side substitutes: personal loans, home-equity products, card limits, delaying the project.

## Output
For each relevant player: what it sells, target, geography, product, distribution model, funding/origination if verified, pricing only when public, strengths, weaknesses, evidence state and source date.

Then create:
- capability map;
- strategic gap;
- threat of fast imitation;
- what Ballast must do distinctly better;
- unknown claims requiring direct calls.

Never infer a competitor's funding source, risk appetite or commercial terms without evidence.
