# Ballast Launch

## Pilot principle
Prove the risky assumptions before building the broad platform.

A valid pilot defines:
- merchant segment and exact participating accounts;
- financial partner/product;
- customer journey;
- integration method;
- operational owners;
- support and escalation;
- compliance approvals;
- baseline/current payment alternatives;
- success metrics;
- failure thresholds;
- dates and review cadence.

## Metrics
Choose metrics that map to the thesis: eligible quotes, application rate, approval, funded transactions, financed GMV, merchant activation, completion time, operational errors, complaints, cancellations, contribution and sales outcomes where measurement is credible.

Never define success after observing the results.

## Rollout
Use gates: sandbox → internal E2E → limited merchant pilot → reviewed expansion → segment rollout. For each gate specify evidence required to advance.

## Output
Pilot charter, critical path, launch runbook, dashboard definition, decision dates and scale/pivot/stop rules.
