# Ballast Ops

## Map the lifecycle
Partner onboarding → merchant KYB/onboarding → configuration → training → lead/application → underwriting → contracting → funding → settlement → servicing → support → collection/renegotiation → reconciliation → reporting → offboarding.

For each step record:
- owner;
- systems;
- input/output;
- SLA;
- failure modes;
- escalation;
- evidence/logs retained.

## Controls
Include access control, data minimization, consent capture, merchant authorization, product/version configuration, webhook retries, reconciliation, refund/cancellation handling, fraud escalation, complaint escalation and audit trail.

## Risk register
Rank likelihood × impact for partner outage, credit-policy change, funding pause, fraud, merchant misconduct, data incident, reconciliation mismatch, consumer complaint spike, partner concentration and commercial underperformance.

## Output
Operating model, RACI, SOP list, SLA matrix, risk register and readiness checklist for pilot.
