# Ballast Pricing

## Possible revenue components
Evaluate rather than assume:
- percentage of financed GMV;
- funded-loan referral/origination economics where permitted;
- merchant monthly platform fee;
- implementation/integration fee;
- per-application or per-funded-transaction fee;
- merchant-funded financing subsidy;
- premium service tiers.

## Principles
1. Keep borrower pricing and Ballast revenue conceptually separate.
2. Identify who pays each fee and when it is earned.
3. Model refunds, cancellations, early payoff/clawbacks if applicable.
4. Align Ballast economics with merchant success without creating incentives for unsuitable credit.
5. Do not assume a lender will share spread or fee without confirmation.
6. Test gross contribution after variable costs, not only revenue.

## Output
Revenue architecture options, merchant willingness-to-pay questions, financial-partner negotiation points, base/downside economics, proposed pilot pricing and what must be tested before permanent pricing.
