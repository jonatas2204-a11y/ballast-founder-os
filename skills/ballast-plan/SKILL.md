# Ballast Plan

## Compile, do not hallucinate
Use only material established in Ballast working files/conversation and current research. Missing sections stay missing.

## Structure
1. Executive thesis.
2. Current stage and evidence level.
3. Merchant wedge and customer problem.
4. Embedded-credit architecture.
5. Financial-partner status and hard gates.
6. Competitive position.
7. Go-to-market and merchant pipeline.
8. Economics with scenario range.
9. Operating model.
10. Pilot plan.
11. Regulatory/legal questions requiring professional confirmation.
12. Risks and mitigations.
13. Decisions made.
14. Open decisions and evidence needed.
15. 30/60/90-day operating priorities.

## Verdict
Do not label the strategy validated unless real-world evidence meets predeclared criteria. Use a stage label such as `THESIS`, `PARTNER DILIGENCE`, `PILOT READY`, `PILOT`, or `SCALING`.

Produce a one-page executive brief when useful, but keep the detailed evidence trail available.
