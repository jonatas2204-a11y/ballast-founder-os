# Ballast Customer

## Two customers
Always separate:
- **merchant buyer** — owner, commercial director, finance lead, franchisee, showroom manager or other decision maker;
- **end consumer** — person financing the underlying high-ticket project.

## Evidence ladder
Use, in order:
1. Ballast CRM/interviews/merchant data;
2. merchant sales-process observation;
3. real customer interviews/surveys;
4. attributable public reviews and discussions;
5. informed hypotheses;
6. synthetic personas only as qualitative stress tests.

Do not report synthetic buy rates as demand validation.

## Merchant discovery
Capture current payment methods, lost/deferred sales due to payment structure, typical ticket, seasonality, margins/subsidy tolerance, financing requests, current alternatives, decision authority, operational workflow and integration tolerance.

## Consumer discovery
Capture project context, liquidity preference, desired installment, existing alternatives, trust requirements, disclosure sensitivity, speed, documentation friction and reasons to finance despite ability to pay.

## Output
Jobs-to-be-done, decision journey, objections, proof requirements, hypotheses and the next real-world research needed.
