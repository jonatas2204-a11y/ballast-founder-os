# Ballast Market

## Objective
Find markets where merchant need, financing usefulness, distribution economics and partner credit appetite overlap.

## Segment frame
For each candidate segment evaluate:
- typical project/ticket range (fact or estimate explicitly labelled);
- transaction frequency;
- fragmentation vs chain concentration;
- class A/B exposure;
- financing relevance (liquidity preservation, project acceleration, budget expansion);
- existing payment substitutes;
- merchant gross-margin sensitivity to subsidy;
- ease of finding/activating decision makers;
- operational suitability for a credit journey;
- likely partner underwriting constraints.

## Sizing
Prefer bottom-up:
`addressable merchants × eligible annual sales/quotes × realistic financing penetration × financed ticket`

Show formula, sources and low/base/high assumptions. Do not present a single false-precision TAM.

## Output
Ranked segment table, evidence gaps, market thesis, top wedges, segments to avoid now and the next evidence-generating actions.
