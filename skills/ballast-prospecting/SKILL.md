# Ballast Prospecting

## Target profile
Prioritize merchants with:
- high project ticket and financing usefulness;
- class A/B customer exposure;
- meaningful sales volume or repeatable project flow;
- decision-maker accessibility;
- a sales journey where financing can be inserted;
- no publicly verified dedicated financing solution, or an existing solution with a clearly identifiable gap;
- Greater São Paulo presence for initial wedge unless another geography is requested.

Do not claim “no financing” from silence. Use evidence-policy language.

## Research
For each company verify official name/brand, segment, locations, offering, positioning, scale indicators, current payment/financing evidence, official website and the strongest public contact channel. Decision-maker names must be verified.

## Score
Use six 0–10 dimensions from `schemas/lead.schema.json` and run `scripts/lead_score.py` when practical. Explain scores with evidence; the script ranks, it does not create evidence.

## Account brief
Return: company, priority, why-now hypothesis, ticket/use-case estimate labelled as such, financing status, likely buyer role, verified contacts, custom opening angle, discovery questions and next action.

No invented WhatsApp, email pattern or executive.
