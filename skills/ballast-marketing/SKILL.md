# Ballast Marketing

## Split the audiences
B2B merchant acquisition and B2C borrower activation are different systems.

### B2B
Use target-account evidence to create positioning, proof, channel strategy, content, events/associations, outbound support and sales enablement.

### B2C / point of sale
Design merchant-facing language and financing UX around clarity, trust, eligibility, project affordability and liquidity preservation. Do not shame the borrower or imply financial weakness.

## Message hierarchy
Every claim must map to one of:
- verified capability;
- verified customer evidence;
- clearly labelled hypothesis to test.

No fabricated conversion improvement, approval rate, savings, lender count or customer testimonial.

## Economics
Use Ballast economics to constrain CAC and channel spend. If LTV or retention is unknown, do not invent a CAC ceiling.

## Output
Positioning, message house, channel plan, content/outbound experiments, funnel metrics and a 30-day test plan where useful.
