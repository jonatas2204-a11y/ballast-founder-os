# Ballast Sales

## Account-specific first
Before writing outreach, identify the account's business model, likely financing use case, current payment evidence, target decision-maker role and one relevant contextual observation.

## Outreach structure
Use:
1. relevance trigger specific to account;
2. commercially meaningful problem/hypothesis;
3. Ballast mechanism in one sentence;
4. low-friction CTA.

Avoid generic praise, fake familiarity, invented metrics, urgency or pretending a partnership already exists.

## Discovery
For merchants ask about ticket, sales cycle, payment methods, financing requests, lost/deferred deals, current alternatives, margins/subsidy tolerance, decision process, customer profile, operational integration and success criteria.

For financial partners use the hard-gate questions in `decision-gates.md` first.

## Follow-up
Each follow-up should add a reason to reply: a sharper use case, a relevant finding, a proposed pilot outline or a specific question. No empty “checking in.”

## Output
Channel-specific copy for email/WhatsApp/LinkedIn/phone, discovery agenda, objection handling, CRM next step and follow-up cadence.
