# Evidence policy

Every material claim used in a recommendation should carry one of these states:

- `VERIFIED_PUBLIC` — supported by a current, attributable public source.
- `USER_PROVIDED` — explicitly supplied by the user or Ballast documentation.
- `ESTIMATE` — calculated or inferred from known inputs; methodology must be stated.
- `ASSUMPTION` — intentionally assumed for modeling or scenario planning.
- `UNKNOWN` — not established yet.

## Rules

1. Absence of public evidence is not evidence of absence.
2. Never write that a merchant has no financing solution merely because one was not found. Use: “No dedicated financing solution was identified in the public sources reviewed.”
3. Never invent a telephone number, email address, executive name, price, APR, approval rate, funding limit, take rate or partner capability.
4. Separate company facts from market estimates and model assumptions.
5. Record source date for claims that can change.
6. For high-stakes partner decisions, prefer primary sources: official websites, regulatory records, contracts, documentation and direct commercial confirmation.
7. Simulated customers are qualitative hypothesis generators only. Their purchase rates are not evidence of market conversion.
8. When evidence conflicts, preserve the conflict and identify the stronger source instead of silently choosing one.

## Claim format

For internal working files, prefer:

`Claim | Evidence state | Source | Date | Confidence | Decision impact`

Unknowns with high decision impact become due-diligence questions.
