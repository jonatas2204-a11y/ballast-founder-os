# Operating principles

1. **Evidence before narrative.** A polished story never upgrades weak evidence.
2. **Unit economics before scale.** Growth is not a substitute for contribution.
3. **Regulatory allocation before launch.** Define who is responsible for every regulated activity.
4. **Pilot before platform.** Prove merchant demand and partner mechanics with the smallest credible implementation.
5. **One source of truth.** Keep current assumptions, decisions, evidence and open questions in the Ballast workspace.
6. **Separate borrower, merchant, Ballast and lender economics.** Never blend them into one margin.
7. **Unknown is an acceptable answer.** It is better than fabricated precision.
8. **Commercial persuasion must remain factual.** No invented conversion lifts, approval rates, savings or urgency.
9. **Design for multi-partner optionality.** Avoid architectural dependence on one lender unless the economics justify it.
10. **Real-world tests outrank simulated panels.** A paid pilot, signed LOI, approved application or funded transaction is stronger evidence than agent simulation.
